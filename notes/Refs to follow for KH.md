Paper 1 deals with Static properties, while 2 deals with Paper 2 deals with dynamics of Majorana fermions. Unsure if relevant.

Chaloupka, Jackelli, and Khaliullin have a paper (https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.105.027204) discussing phase diagrams in the KH model. Hinges on exact diagonalization. Might be a relevant study.

https://journals.aps.org/prb/pdf/10.1103/PhysRevB.84.165414 has a more detailed derivation of the physical solutions in the Lieb ground state. Reference from Mandal's review. Keeping here in case I need to reference.

Kimchi and Vishwanath have a paper (https://arxiv.org/pdf/1303.3290) discussing some exactly solvable point of the KH model, using something called _Klein duality_. I do not know if this will be relevant.
